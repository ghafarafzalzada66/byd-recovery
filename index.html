Frequently asked questions

Questions before you book?

How much does emergency breakdown recovery cost?

Emergency recovery call-outs start from £50 according to the current website information. Final pricing depends on location, vehicle, time and job requirements.

Are you available 24 hours a day?

BYD Recovery advertises 24/7 breakdown recovery and roadside assistance, subject to operational availability.

How quickly can you reach me?

The previous website stated a typical local response of around 20–40 minutes. Actual arrival depends on traffic, exact location, demand and vehicle availability.

Do you transport vehicles throughout the UK?

Yes. Planned vehicle transportation is advertised throughout the UK, with mainland European transport available by quotation and subject to availability.

Can you recover a car with locked wheels or that won't start?

Recovery may be possible using winches, skates and specialist loading equipment, depending on the vehicle and situation.

Do you offer trade vehicle transportation?

Yes. The service is intended for garages, dealerships, bodyshops, vehicle traders, fleet operators and auction buyers, with trade accounts subject to approval.
